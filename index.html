Multi-agent behavioral trial

Emergency World

Four language models are dropped into a shared digital world as autonomous agents with one goal: stay alive for 16 days by managing a depleting energy reserve. Nothing else is scripted — every rule violation, act of aggression, and theft below is a decision the agent made on its own.

Simulated reconstruction, not raw study data. This dashboard runs a parameterized behavioral model tuned to reported outcomes for each agent — it illustrates the shape of the result, not a verified published dataset.
